Pat Garrett & Billy the Kid is a western about aging legends, friendship under pressure, betrayal, and death arriving as part of history. Pat Garrett represents law, authority, survival, and compromise. Billy the Kid represents youth, rebellion, myth, and refusal to fully surrender. Their story is not only about crime or heroism; it is about what happens when one world replaces another.

In the film's atmosphere, dust, badges, guns, sunsets, and empty landscapes become symbols of farewell. A badge can suggest authority, but a fallen badge can suggest that authority has lost its certainty. A western door can be the entrance to a saloon, a home, a jail, or a final passage. This project uses that visual language to design a personal door: a symbolic threshold between what the user has been and what they are becoming.
